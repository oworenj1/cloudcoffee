☁️ Our Story

More Than Just Coffee

CloudCoffee was born from a simple dream — to create a cozy corner in the sky where every cup tells a story. Inspired by Korean café culture and the serene beauty of clouds, we craft drinks that feel like a warm hug on a cloudy day.

From our signature Iced Lattes to dreamy Blended Frappes, every drink is made with freshly-sourced beans, premium milk, and a whole lot of love.
